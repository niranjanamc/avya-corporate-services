An award is more than an object—it is a lasting symbol of pride and achievement. We combine precision 3D subsurface laser engraving, optical polishing, and custom silk packaging for awards that employees treasure for a lifetime.

Optical K9 Crystal & Glass Sculptures

Manufactured from high-density optical grade K9 crystal, our trophies feature diamond-cut facets that catch and refract ambient light brilliantly, making every presentation moment on stage extraordinary.

  • ✦ Flawless bubble-free optical K9 crystal with precision beveled edges
  • ✦ High-resolution 2D and 3D sub-surface laser engraving inside the crystal
  • ✦ Metallic champagne gold, frosted crystal, and full-color UV printed bases
  • ✦ Custom bespoke shapes: Flames, spires, stars, geometric pillars, and company logos
  • ✦ Individual protective presentation cases lined in rich royal satin

Cast Metal Medals & Hardwood Plaques

For sports tournaments, corporate hackathons, and long-service commemorations, we produce solid die-cast metal medals and seasoned teakwood/mahogany plaques with etched brass plates.

  • ✦ Heavyweight die-cast zinc alloy and brass medals with 3D embossed reliefs
  • ✦ Antique gold, shiny gold, silver, and antique bronze electroplated finishes
  • ✦ Custom woven satin neck ribbons with dye-sublimated corporate logos
  • ✦ Seasoned polished hardwood plaques with laser-etched black-and-gold plates
  • ✦ Desk-standing brass commemorative paperweights and executive coins
