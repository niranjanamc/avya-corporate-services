Privacy Policy & Document Confidentiality

How AVYA Corporate Services safeguards your enterprise data, statutory documentation, and sensitive identity records with uncompromising physical and digital security.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Reviewed: November 2026

1. Commitment to Enterprise Confidentiality

AVYA Corporate Services ("AVYA", "we", "us", or "our") operates as a trusted single-window partner providing corporate operations, statutory liaison (RTO, Notary, Passport), logistics, and vendor management services. We recognize that our corporate clients entrust us with sensitive business information, employee identity records, and high-value physical documentation. We maintain the highest standards of data protection in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

2. Information We Collect & Process

Depending on the specific service engaged by your organization, we may collect and process:

  • Corporate Contact Data: Authorized representative name, official email address, corporate phone number, company name, office address, and GSTIN.
  • Vehicle & Statutory Records (RTO Services): Vehicle Registration Certificates (RC), hypothecation agreements, insurance policies, emission certificates, fitness records, and owner identification.
  • Legal & Contractual Documents (Stamp & Notary): Commercial contracts, lease agreements, affidavits, board resolutions, power of attorney documents, and authorized signatory identity proofs required for notarisation.
  • Passport & Travel Liaison Records: Application forms, appointment acknowledgments, address proofs, and supporting documents strictly required by the Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) / Regional Passport Office.
  • Hardware & Logistics Information: Laptop serial numbers, IT equipment specifications, employee transit delivery addresses, and proof-of-delivery signatures.

3. Purpose of Processing

All collected information is processed solely for executing the authorized services requested by your organization, including liaising with statutory authorities (RTO, PSK, Notaries Public), dispatching IT hardware, fulfilling CSR distributions, and rendering consolidated monthly billing. We never sell, rent, commercialize, or share your data with third-party advertisers or unauthorized external entities.

4. Document Custody & Physical Security

Physical original documents (such as original RC smart cards, stamped deed papers, and identity proofs) are subject to rigorous chain-of-custody protocols:

  • Doorstep handovers are conducted exclusively by verified AVYA field officers using tamper-evident, sealed envelopes with signed receipt slips.
  • Original documents are retained in secure, locked custody only for the minimal duration required for government department verification and endorsement.
  • Upon statutory processing completion, original documents and receipts are promptly returned to the client's designated nodal officer.

5. Data Retention & Secure Disposal

We retain operational and billing records only as mandated by applicable statutory, tax (GST), and audit obligations. Intermediate digital scans or copies collected strictly for application filing are purged securely from our active processing systems upon verified delivery of the completed statutory deliverable.

6. Disclosure to Statutory Authorities

Information and supporting documents are submitted strictly to official government bodies (such as Regional Transport Offices, Passport Seva Kendras, and registered Advocates/Notaries Public) as legally required to execute your requested filings. Such disclosures are governed by official governmental procedures and statutory mandates.
