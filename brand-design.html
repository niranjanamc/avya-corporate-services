Pantone Matching & Merchandise Guidelines

Color deviation degrades corporate identity. We match your corporate brand guides against official Pantone Matching System (PMS) formulas for coated, uncoated, metallic, and textile dyes.

  • ✦ Exact Pantone PMS formula ink mixing for offset and screen printing
  • ✦ Comprehensive Swag & Merchandise Brand Style Guides for enterprise marketing teams
  • ✦ Minimum logo clearance, negative space rules, and embroidery stitch calibrations
  • ✦ Vectorization and resolution enhancement of legacy logos and raster assets
