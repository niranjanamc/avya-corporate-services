RC Transfers & Hypothecation Cancellation

When company lease vehicles are bought out by employees, or when corporate vehicles are sold or re-assigned, ownership transfer and loan clearance must be promptly recorded with the RTO.

  • RC Ownership Transfer: Corporate-to-Individual and Individual-to-Corporate transfers with complete Form 29/30 documentation.
  • Hypothecation Cancellation (HP Termination): Endorsement removal from RC smart card upon loan closure using Form 35 and Bank NOC.
  • Duplicate RC Smart Card: Fast replacement for lost, damaged, or mutilated Registration Certificates.

Commercial Vehicle Fitness Certificates (FC)

Operating corporate employee transport cabs, traveler vans, or staff commuter buses requires valid, up-to-date Fitness Certificates under the Motor Vehicles Act.

  • Inspection Coordination: Pre-inspection documentation check and physical slot coordination at RTO test tracks.
  • Timely Renewals: Proactive renewal reminders and processing to prevent heavy daily statutory fines and vehicle impoundment.
  • Speed Governor & Tracking Compliance: Assistance with mandatory AIS-140 GPS and speed governor certifications.

No Objection Certificates (NOC) & Driving Licences

Relocating employees across Indian states or transferring corporate vehicles across state borders requires an official RTO No Objection Certificate (Form 28) and clearance certificates.

  • Inter-State NOC (Form 28): Comprehensive police and NCRB clearance coordination for vehicle movement out of Karnataka.
  • Driving Licence Services: Corporate desk assistance for driving licence renewals, address changes, international permits (IDP), and badge renewals.
  • Road Tax & Penalty Advisory: Clear guidance on life-time tax payments and statutory refunds for inter-state transfers.
