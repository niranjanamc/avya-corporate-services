Terms of Engagement & Service Framework

Standard terms governing corporate operations, statutory liaison, facilities coordination, logistics handling, and institutional invoicing with AVYA Corporate Services.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

1. Scope of Engagement

These Terms of Engagement govern all services provided by AVYA Corporate Services ("AVYA", "Service Provider") to corporate clients, institutions, and businesses ("Client"). Services encompass Business Support (daily florists, periodicals, local vendors), RTO Vehicle Liaison, Stamp Paper & Notary Facilitation, CSR Supply Kits, Corporate Passport Assistance, and Courier & IT Asset Movement. Specific service scopes, pricing schedules, and deliverables are formalized via approved proposals, Purchase Orders (PO), or Master Service Agreements (MSA).

2. Client Authorization & Statutory Representation

For services involving governmental and statutory bodies (such as Regional Transport Offices, Notaries Public, and Passport Seva Kendras):

  • The Client represents and warrants that all documentation, vehicle information, identity records, and representations provided to AVYA are genuine, accurate, and lawfully obtained.
  • The Client shall provide requisite authorization letters, board resolutions, and Form 28/29/30/34/35 signatures in compliance with the Motor Vehicles Act and rules thereunder.
  • AVYA acts strictly as an authorized facilitator and corporate liaison. AVYA does not fabricate, alter, or misrepresent records before any public authority.

3. Government Timelines & Statutory Dependencies

While AVYA commits to expeditious filing and proactive on-ground follow-up, final statutory issuance turnaround times (such as RC endorsements, fitness certificates, hypothecation cancellations, police verification clearances, and passport dispatches) remain dependent upon government department workflows, server availability (e.g., Vahan / Sarathi portals), and public inspection schedules. SLA timelines communicated by AVYA represent target working-day estimates based on customary administrative procedures.

4. IT Asset Transit & Logistics Liability

For employee laptop dispatches, reverse logistics, and asset movement:

  • AVYA enforces high-grade tamper-evident anti-shock packaging and verified consignment barcoding.
  • Consignments transit via tier-1 domestic cargo networks under tracked air/surface waybills.
  • Declared transit insurance coverage is strongly advised for high-value electronic consignments. AVYA's direct carriage liability is capped at the standard carrier carriage terms unless formal declared-value insurance is explicitly booked prior to dispatch.

5. Quotations, Pricing & Government Outlays

Commercial quotations clearly distinguish between: (a) statutory non-refundable government duties/fees (e.g., Karnataka state e-stamp duty, RTO smart card fees, cess, challans) and (b) AVYA professional coordination and service facilitation charges. Official statutory fee receipts issued by respective departments are submitted along with invoices.

6. Invoicing, GST & Payment Terms

All invoices are issued under GSTIN 29APAPV7401D1ZY with appropriate SAC classifications (9985, 9987, 9968, 9983). Monthly retainers and completed milestone invoices are payable within 15 to 30 calendar days of invoice presentation, as specified in the service agreement, via NEFT/RTGS or official corporate banking channels.

7. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

AVYA maintains strict confidentiality regarding client operations, employee identity records, vendor rates, and proprietary data. Both parties agree to protect proprietary information using the same degree of care exercised for their own confidential assets.

8. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms and all corporate engagements shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with the engagement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
